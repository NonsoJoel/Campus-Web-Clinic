**PRODUCT REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT (PRD)**

**PRODUCT NAME:** CAMPUS WEB CLINIC

**PRODUCT OVERVIEW**

An online web portal aimed at making medical health processes more seamless and convenient for patients and hospital staffs by enabling the booking of appointments and filling out of basic symptoms beforehand to help improve the treatment process.

**PROBLEM STATEMENT**

Many medical help centers are usually met with the following problems:

- Overcrowding and long queues
- Delays in services
- Inability to get the necessary treatment
- Time wasting
- Patients being brought to hospital for minor symptoms due to lack of proper information

**BUSINESS OBJECTIVES**

The business is aimed at you the system to:

- Increase the efficiency and accuracy of medical treatments to help satisfy the needs of as many patients as possible.
- Making medical health procedures more convenient and less time consuming.
- Minimize human errors in the storing and sorting of patients medical records and enable easy retrieval.

**TARGET USERS**

- Patients
- Hospital staff members
- Doctors and Consultants

**STAKEHOLDERS**

- Patients
- Hospital Staffs
- Hospital administrator
- Board of directors
- Pharmaceutical staff
- Receptionist

**USER STORIES**

- As a patient, I want a way to make medical processes more seamless, so that I cannot spend more time than I intended.
- As a receptionist, I want an easy way to register new patients, so that I can be able to store records more accurately.
- As a doctor, I want a way to a get patient's symptoms earlier before an appointment, so that I can be able to administer the proper treatment.
- As a receptionist, I want a secure record database, so that all patient records can be stored and kept hidden from unauthorized personnel.
- As a patient, I want to be able to book and have appointments without being physically present, so that it will be more convenient for me if I'm unable to be physically present.

**FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

- Patients and Staffs should be able to register and log into the portal.
- Patients should be able to book appointments based on desired date, time and doctor/consultant.
- Informing of patients of free time slots for appointment booking.
- It should be able to give patients access to online doctor's appointment.
- It should be able to store patient records accurately and securely.
- Allow the easy retrieval of patient's past medical records.
- In cases of hospital transfers, it should be able to enable an interconnection of patient medical histories with other hospitals.
- The system should give patients a breakdown of all the medical services present in the hospital.

**NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

- User authentication – passwords, usernames, hospital IDs and two-step verification
- User data privacy and security
- Database should be able to handle large amount of users simultaneously
- Reliable and seamless
- Simplicity – User friendly interface and easy to understand instructions
- Maintainable and should be able to allow easy updates and error fixes
- Fast response time
- Offline access to the system and user records

**MVP FEATURES**

- User registration
- Appointment booking
- Patient prescription details
- Patient billing breakdown
- AI and human customer services
- Details on past medical history
- Details on available medical services
- Reporting of patient symptoms

**ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA**

- During login when a correct password and username is inputted you are given access but when an incorrect password or username is inputted is shows an error and denies access.
- When a new staff is employed and registered, their details should be saved properly and displayed appropriately when called upon.
- Billing should be properly calculated according to the services administered.
- It should be able to display an accurate consultation status for a certain consultant – whether the consultant's schedule is free for a consultation or taken by another patient.
- It should be able to bring up the right medical record for the right patient when called upon by their patient ID.
